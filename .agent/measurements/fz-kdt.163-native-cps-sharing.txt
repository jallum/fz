fz-kdt.163 native CPS sharing census
=====================================

Reproduce
---------

Run from the repository root:

    cargo test --lib compiler2::drive_test::measure_native_cps_sharing_corpus -- --ignored --exact --nocapture

The retained ignored test selects every direct child of `fixtures2/` and
`fixtures2/behavior/` whose extension is `.fz` and whose source contains
`fn main()`. It captures the closed native program immediately before and after
sharing, compiles both, and prints totals plus every excluded and moving path.
The selection is deliberately syntactic and deterministic: 510 candidates =
474 resolved + 2 panics + 34 expected fatal/unresolved fixtures.

Corpus result on the combined PR #110 parent (2026-09-04)
--------------------------

- resolved fixtures: 474
- paired pre/post codegen successes: 472
- semantic executable entries: 5,760
- same-function sibling groups: 797
- initially equivalent graph classes: 36, covering 52 redundant roots
- physical executable roots: 5,760 before; 5,707 after (-53)
- owned continuations: 8,625 before; 8,487 after (-138)
- all native bodies: 15,760 before; 15,569 after (-191)
- compiled native text: 3,459,136 bytes before; 3,412,776 after (-46,360)
- static `MakeClosure` instructions: 118 before and after
- deterministic sharing work: 204 ownership-index passes over 18,406 bodies
  and 17,608 structurally reachable graph-body roles; 6,767 graph comparisons
  and 6,071 body comparisons
- observed warm sharing: 79,026 us
- observed warm paired codegen: 12,882 -> 12,361 ms

The combined-stack sample removed 521 ms of paired codegen work for 79 ms of
sharing. Elapsed samples are corroborative, not thresholds. The primary work
proof is deterministic: codegen sees 191 fewer bodies and 46,360 fewer bytes,
while programs without sibling candidates perform zero sharing work and each
remaining pass performs one bounded inventory index with exact work counters.
The integer counters are test-only fields and compile out of production builds;
they measure the retained algorithm without adding runtime observation work.

Continuation roles come from first-reachable completed-IR control-edge paths,
independent of helper construction and body-inventory order. An owned body with
no such path makes its graph conservatively ineligible rather than receiving a
temporal fallback role. The structural negative test pins that rule, and
fz-tfn.27 owns deleting dead branch/resume helpers at their emitter. The indexer
rejects duplicate body, clause, or structural graph roles instead of silently
choosing one.

The first comparison implementation was rejected before delivery. It rebuilt
owned graphs by scanning the full body list for each candidate comparison:
4,924 graph comparisons produced 5,659 and 5,698 body comparisons on two
identical corpus runs because `HashMap` iteration changed the early-exit order.
That version spent about 1,516 ms while its paired run avoided only 1,179 ms of
code generation. The retained implementation instead builds one ownership
index per fixed-point pass and compares bodies in canonical role order.

The fixed point removes one additional root after equivalent callees have
shared, hence 52 roots are equivalent initially and 53 are absent from the
final physical inventory. Semantic entries and constructions are unchanged.

Controls
--------

The boxed reducer witness begins with two distinct, disjoint native graphs,
including their continuations. Their only rich-type difference is on a
`ValueRef` variable proved to be used solely as an indirect callee. Sharing
retains both semantic activations and both predicate construction words while
mapping them to the first physical graph. The direct control `mk(double)` /
`mk(triple)` remains two graphs with distinct grounded local targets.

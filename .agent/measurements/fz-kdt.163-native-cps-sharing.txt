fz-kdt.163 native CPS sharing census
=====================================

Reproduce
---------

Run from the repository root:

    cargo test --lib compiler2::drive_test::measure_native_cps_sharing_corpus -- --ignored --exact --nocapture

The retained ignored test selects every direct child of `fixtures2/` and
`fixtures2/behavior/` whose extension is `.fz` and whose source contains
`fn main()`. It captures the closed native program immediately before and after
sharing, compiles both, and prints totals plus every excluded and moving path.
The selection is deliberately syntactic and deterministic: 502 candidates =
467 resolved + 2 panics + 33 expected fatal/unresolved fixtures.

Corpus result (2026-09-03)
--------------------------

- resolved fixtures: 467
- paired pre/post codegen successes: 465
- semantic executable entries: 5,338
- same-function sibling groups: 662
- initially equivalent graph classes: 40, covering 56 redundant roots
- physical executable roots: 5,338 before; 5,281 after (-57)
- owned continuations: 8,356 before; 8,220 after (-136)
- all native bodies: 14,989 before; 14,796 after (-193)
- compiled native text: 3,483,036 bytes before; 3,438,764 after (-44,272)
- static `MakeClosure` instructions: 148 before and after
- deterministic sharing work: 488 ownership-index passes over 19,599 bodies
  and 17,417 structurally reachable graph-body roles; 4,751 graph comparisons and 4,499 body
  comparisons
- observed clean warm sharing passes: 90,192 us and 84,513 us
- observed clean warm paired codegen passes: 16,986 -> 16,816 ms and
  15,662 -> 14,966 ms
- isolated cold target: 63 s compilation plus 120.05 s census, 184.69 s
  end to end, exit 0, and 2.386 GB maximum resident set size
- isolated cold paired codegen pass: 19,535 -> 19,590 ms
  (elapsed samples are corroborative, not thresholds)

The clean warm samples removed 170 ms and 696 ms of paired codegen work for
90 ms and 85 ms of sharing. The cold sample varied by 55 ms in the other
direction while two full codegen passes each took about 19.5 seconds; that
noise is retained rather than presented as a speedup. The primary work proof
is deterministic: codegen sees 193 fewer bodies and 44,272 fewer bytes, while
sharing performs one bounded inventory index per pass and the exact comparison
counters repeat. Elapsed samples are a coarse systematic-regression check only.
The integer counters are test-only fields and compile out of production builds;
they measure the retained algorithm without adding runtime observation work.

Continuation roles come from first-reachable completed-IR control-edge paths,
independent of helper construction and body-inventory order. Fifty-nine owned
body occurrences across the 488 fixed-point passes have no such path, so their
graphs are conservatively ineligible rather than receiving a temporal fallback
role. An exhaustive audit found that every omitted helper is a generated
`__branch_N` or `__resume_N` body with no control edge, construction word, or
published native/callable-boundary reference anywhere in its program: genuinely
dead native emission, tracked for deletion at its emitter by fz-tfn.27. The
indexer rejects duplicate body, clause, or structural graph roles instead of
silently choosing one.

The first comparison implementation was rejected before delivery. It rebuilt
owned graphs by scanning the full body list for each candidate comparison:
4,924 graph comparisons produced 5,659 and 5,698 body comparisons on two
identical corpus runs because `HashMap` iteration changed the early-exit order.
That version spent about 1,516 ms while its paired run avoided only 1,179 ms of
code generation. The retained implementation instead builds one ownership
index per fixed-point pass and compares bodies in canonical role order.

The fixed point removes one additional root after equivalent callees have
shared, hence 56 roots are equivalent initially and 57 are absent from the
final physical inventory. Semantic entries and constructions are unchanged.

Controls
--------

The boxed reducer witness begins with two distinct, disjoint native graphs,
including their continuations. Their only rich-type difference is on a
`ValueRef` variable proved to be used solely as an indirect callee. Sharing
retains both semantic activations and both predicate construction words while
mapping them to the first physical graph. The direct control `mk(double)` /
`mk(triple)` remains two graphs with distinct grounded local targets.

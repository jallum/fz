fz-tfn.9 immutable product-payload sharing
===========================================

Probe
-----

A temporary test-only counter wrapped `ProductValue::clone` and counted a deep
clone whenever the value held a root answer, symbolic backend, ABI,
materialized executable, runtime demand, or callable-construction owner. A
second probe counted the whole-demand clones at the typed
`read_runtime_demand` boundary that the first counter missed. Backend packaging
also cloned that demand once per packaged executable; its exact executable
inventory supplies that count. Each fixture was driven alone with
`--test-threads=1`.

00420_enum_take_drop_split                           9,605         10,562         204        7,912             22,618                            50,901       0
fn main(), do: 1 + 2                                    106             97           3  not measured       not measured                               206       0

The corrected before column is deliberately a lower bound, not a claimed
global total. Review found a further uninstrumented deep clone when the old
demand cone split a settled answer between its read view and publication. That
site was removed by retaining `Rc` in the settled cone. The after column is
exact for the instrumented wrapper, typed-read, packaging, and 00420 Jacobi
sites; the permanent pointer-identity test covers the now-shared retained path.

The follow-up `Rc::make_mut` detector measured the table's 7,912 guaranteed
copy-on-write clones while settling 00420: each derived answer was stored in
both the read view and the final iterate before the next round tried to mutate
the read view. Keeping one frozen Jacobi view and supplying the formula's
own-demand overlay separately reduced that exact site to 0. The detector and
its test were removed after the green measurement. The column was not measured
on the small fixture, and the counters were introduced at different review
points, so the sum remains an observed lower bound rather than a simultaneous
global clone census.

A final overlay probe measured 22,618 redundant whole-vector copies on 00420:
one copy moving the already-owned `RuntimeDemandOwnInput` into the formula
snapshot, then another copy retaining its input-demand vector for the final
join. The formula now consumes the overlay into a production-non-`Clone`
snapshot and borrows that vector through the join, reducing both sites to 0.
Like the Jacobi measurement, this was measured after the preceding redesign;
the 50,901 figure is the sum of directly observed eliminated operations across
the review snapshots, not a claim that one baseline run performed every
operation simultaneously.

Both counters and their probe tests were deleted after recording the result.
Permanent tests use `Rc::ptr_eq` from the memo through materialization, ABI,
symbolic backend, and packaged backend output instead of retaining measurement
scaffolding.

Resulting ownership
-------------------

- A producer allocates each large product payload once. ProductValue, the
  typed PullSession inventory, ProductMemo, downstream typed products, and
  cache hits retain the same `Rc`.
- ABI products retain their materialized input and callable-construction
  owners. Symbolic backend products retain their ABI input. Materialized, ABI,
  symbolic backend, and packaged backend products all retain the memoized
  runtime-demand allocation.
- Root answers and World retain one `Rc<BackendProgram>`; macro consumers keep
  that handle. World stores and returns `Rc<NativeProgram>`.
- All members of a recursive completion retain one validated
  `Rc<ProductDependencies>` snapshot.

`Rc` is sufficient: PullSession and World are single-thread owned, already use
`Rc` for facts and ordered product values, and do not satisfy a `Send`/`Sync`
handoff. `Arc` would add atomic reference-count traffic without making a real
ownership path valid.

Structural equality remains the movement rule. Separately allocated equal
payloads preserve the existing generation; changed payload content advances
it. Same-handle equality short-circuits on `Rc::ptr_eq` before structural
fallback. Product keys, dependency contents, and group validation are
unchanged.

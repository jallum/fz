    fz-go4.18.28.13: prune the transport consult ledger to live closure membership
    
    record_transport_closure_consult built a full cross-product of consulted
    executables x closure members and was never pruned when
    drop_solved_transport_closure/clear_solved_transport_closures evicted the
    closure cover, so the ledger grew monotonically for the session (quicksort
    ~420 edges, reverse ~380, never reclaimed).
    
    SolvedTransportClosure now carries its own `consulted` set. Installing a
    closure into the cover (record_solved_transport_closure) installs its
    consult edges as the SAME dense product in lockstep; dropping a closure
    (drop_solved_transport_closure, including the displacement path and the
    epoch-wide clear_solved_transport_closures sweep) retracts exactly those
    edges. clear_solved_transport_closures now hands back the drained closures
    so invalidate_transport_products can defer the retraction until after its
    walk (which still needs the pre-clear ledger to reach every consult
    dependent) finishes.
    
    Kept the dense cross-product form: closures are disjoint by construction
    (a member belongs to at most one live closure), so every edge is owned
    outright by exactly one closure and precise removal needs no reference
    counting. A sparser consulted->representative star risks under-invalidation
    (a consulted fact's movement must retract every member's shape, not just
    one), so density was kept and only the lifetime was fixed.
    
    Added two direct-construction unit tests
    (transport_closure_consult_ledger_prunes_displaced_closure_on_resolve,
    transport_closure_consult_ledger_prunes_on_epoch_invalidation) proving
    edges are retracted precisely on both the disjoint re-solve path and the
    epoch-wide invalidation path; both fail without the fix. Single-shot
    fixture ledger sizes are unchanged (quicksort 420, reverse 380 -- no
    re-solve happens in one compile), and the fixture matrix's pre-existing
    21-name failure set is unchanged. Both .28.5 contract tests
    (compiler2_transport_consult_ledger_displaces_co_members_on_demand_movement,
    compiler2_transport_world_fact_ledger_displaces_co_members_on_return_type_movement)
    stay green.
